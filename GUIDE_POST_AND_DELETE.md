# SPORTIVA — How to Post & Delete (User Guide)

A beginner-friendly guide to sharing content on the SPORTIVA community feed — and removing it when you want to. Everything below works **even when you are offline** (your action is saved on your device and sent automatically when you reconnect).

---

## 1. What You Can Share

The **News & Feed** module (`/media-feed/`) lets every logged-in user publish:

| Content | How |
|---|---|
| 📝 Text post | Write a message — a training note, match result, announcement |
| 🖼️ Photo post | Text + an uploaded image |
| 🎬 Video / Short (Reel) | A YouTube/TikTok link, or tick **"Short"** to mark it as a reel |
| #️⃣ Hashtags | Type tags like `#Football #Training` so others can find your post |
| 📍 Location | City + country shown on the interactive map |

---

## 2. Creating a Post — Step by Step

1. **Log in**, then click **News & Feed** in the top tab bar (on mobile it's in the bottom bar).
2. Click the **"+ Create / Share"** button — it opens `/media-feed/create/`.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Post type** — Moment (photo/text), Match Highlight, Training Clip, Announcement…
   - **Sport** — links your post to a sport category
   - **Title** *(optional)* — a headline
   - **Content** *(required)* — your message
   - **Image** *(optional)* — upload a photo from your device
   - **Video URL** *(optional)* — paste a YouTube or TikTok link
   - **Short / Reel** *(checkbox)* — tick it so the post shows the ▶ Short badge
   - **Hashtags** — e.g. `#Exploit #Speed` (optional, but helps discovery)
   - **Country / City** — where the moment happened
4. Press **Publish**. You'll see a green banner:
   > *"Your moment has been shared with the SPORTIVA community!"*

Your post now appears at the top of the feed and on your profile page.

> **Note:** posts cannot be edited after publishing. To change something, delete the post (section 4) and publish a new one.

---

## 3. Liking & Commenting

- **Like / Unlike:** click the ❤ heart under any post. Clicking again removes your like — nothing is permanent.
- **Comment:** open the post (click the 💬 icon or the post itself), type in the comment box at the bottom, press **Post Comment**.

---

## 4. Deleting Your Post

You can delete **only your own** posts (administrators can delete any, for moderation).

1. Find your post — in the feed or on your profile.
2. Click the small **🗑 trash icon** in the post's top-right corner (next to the Follow button), or open the post and click the red **Delete** button at the top.
3. A confirmation page shows a summary — author, date, likes, comments — and warns:
   > *"All likes and comments on this post will also be permanently removed."*
4. To go ahead, press the red **"Yes, Delete Permanently"** button. To keep the post, press **"Keep My Post"**.
5. You'll be returned to the feed with a confirmation banner.

**That's it** — the post, its image, all likes and all comments are gone forever. There is no trash folder, so be sure before you confirm.

---

## 5. Deleting Your Comment

1. Open the post that contains your comment.
2. Next to your username on the comment, hover and click the small **🗑 trash icon**.
3. The comment disappears immediately — no confirmation page is needed for comments.

You can only delete your **own** comments (administrators excepted).

---

## 6. Offline? No Problem

If you lose your internet connection while using SPORTIVA:

- Pages you visited recently **keep working** from the on-device cache.
- If you submit a form (post, like, comment, event registration…) while offline, you get a friendly **"Action Saved Offline"** page. Your action is queued on your device.
- The moment you're back online, everything is sent automatically and you'll see a toast:
  > *"Offline actions synced — everything is up to date."*

You never need to repeat an action or worry about losing it.

---

## 7. Quick Rules to Remember

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Only the author (or staff) can delete a post/comment | Protects the community from vandalism |
| Deleting is permanent | No undo — read the confirmation page carefully |
| Keep it sports-related & respectful | Content can be removed by moderators |
| Hashtags help you get discovered | Athletes with visible activity rank higher on the scoreboard |
